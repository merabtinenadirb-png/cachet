      Frequently asked questions

      Is it really free?

      Yes. Creating, sending and collecting replies is free with no time limit. Cachet Signature adds exceptional papers and unlimited named envelopes.

      Where is my information stored?

      Nowhere on our side. The invitation is compressed into the link itself. Only people who receive the link can read it.

      How do guests reply?

      They tap “I'll be there”, “Maybe” or “I can't make it”. WhatsApp opens with a message already written to you.

      Can I edit the invitation after sending?

      You can edit and seal it again, which creates a new link to resend. The old link keeps the old version.

      Does the invitation work in Arabic?

      Yes. The invitation displays in French, English or Arabic, with a right-to-left layout.
